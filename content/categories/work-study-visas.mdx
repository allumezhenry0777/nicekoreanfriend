Everyone's Korea plan eventually collides with a visa question: which one, how long, and what does it let you do? These guides map the alphabet soup — D-2, D-4, E-2, F-2-7, the digital nomad visa — and walk you through the procedures people actually face: extending your stay on HiKorea, choosing a language school, and getting legal permission to work part-time as a student. Start with the [visa types overview](/work-study-visas/korean-visa-types-explained), then jump to the guide that matches your situation. Once you're here on a long-term visa, your next stops are the [ARC guide](/living-in-korea/arc-residence-card-guide) and [opening a bank account](/money-banking/open-bank-account-korea).
