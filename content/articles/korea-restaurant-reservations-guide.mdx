A restaurant can appear easy to visit on a map and still require a booking or a queue registration. Start by identifying the **exact branch** and how it admits diners. That small check matters more than downloading every restaurant app.

## Reservation, remote queue or on-site queue?

| Entry method | What it means for your plan | What to confirm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Timed reservation | You book a date, time and party size | Confirmation, deposit, cancellation cutoff and arrival instructions |
| Remote waitlist | You join a queue before reaching the venue | Current position, notification method and when to travel there |
| On-site waitlist | You register after arriving | Whether you may leave briefly and how staff will call you |
| Walk-in | Staff assess available space when you arrive | Whether they can seat your whole party and whether the kitchen is still taking orders |

A queue position is not a promise of a particular seating time. If you have a train or performance afterward, avoid building the schedule around an optimistic wait estimate.

## Find the visitor booking route

[CATCHTABLE Global](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/catchtable-book-restaurants/id1639046576) advertises registration without a Korean phone number, email/card support and management of reservations and waitlists. Search for the restaurant there, but do not assume every venue or time slot will appear.

The [Korea Tourism Organization’s guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=205615) explains that participating venues can offer timed reservations, remote waiting or on-site waiting. A missing reservation button may mean that the restaurant uses another entry method; check its instructions rather than repeatedly attempting signup.

## Before you confirm

Work through this list while the booking page is open:

1. **Branch and address:** match the Korean name and map location to the place you intended to visit.
2. **Date and local time:** save the reservation in Korea time, especially if booking from abroad.
3. **Party details:** confirm the number of diners, seating needs and any restrictions stated by the venue. Ask directly about children, accessibility or dietary needs if the page does not answer your question.
4. **Menu commitment:** check whether you are choosing a particular course or agreeing to a minimum order.
5. **Deposit:** note the total being charged and whether it is per person. Check whether it is refunded, deducted from the meal or handled another way.
6. **Cancellation:** record the exact deadline and no-show terms. Do not infer a free-cancellation policy from another restaurant.
7. **Confirmation:** save the booking reference, restaurant address and contact route. A payment notification alone is not a substitute for checking that the reservation appears in your account.

If the card fails, check the supported payment method and your issuer’s authentication request. Before retrying, look for an existing booking and any pending charge so you can explain the situation to support.

## On the day

Open the booking or waitlist again before leaving. Confirm the branch and route, keep notifications available, and follow any instruction to check in with staff. If you will be late, contact the venue through the booking details; a message does not automatically preserve your table.

For queues, ask staff how quickly you need to return when called. Stay within a distance that makes their instructions realistic. If you decide to eat elsewhere, cancel your queue entry rather than leave staff waiting for your party.

## If the screen only accepts a Korean number

Check that you are using the official global route, where one exists. If the venue uses a different system, ask staff whether they can register an overseas visitor manually. They may offer another method, but there is no universal workaround and you should not use a stranger’s number.

These phrases give staff a clear question:

- **한국 전화번호가 없어요. 어떻게 대기 등록하나요?** — I do not have a Korean phone number. How can I join the waitlist?
- **예약 없이 식사할 수 있나요?** — Can we eat here without a reservation?
- **두 명인데, 얼마나 기다려야 하나요?** — We are two people. About how long is the wait?
- **제 차례가 되면 어떻게 알려 주시나요?** — How will you tell me when it is my turn?

If no supported booking route is available, ask your accommodation whether it can help contact the restaurant, or choose a nearby place accepting walk-ins. Keep a backup especially when dining with children or a group.
