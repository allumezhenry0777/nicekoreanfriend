You have mobile data, but a booking screen still asks for a Korean number. Before buying another SIM, work out **what the service is actually trying to check**. The useful question is not “Does this app work for foreigners?” but “Can I complete this particular task with the documents and payment method I have?”

## Three different things a phone can do

**Internet access** lets you browse and use online services. **SMS reception** lets a service send a code to a supported number. **Korean identity verification (본인인증)** checks registered identity details through a supported method. Having one does not guarantee the other two.

A data-only travel eSIM may leave your home number as the number you use for accounts. If you need home-number texts, check roaming and dual-SIM settings with your carrier before departure. Do not assume your travel data package includes voice calls or incoming SMS.

## Choose a route for the task

| What you want to do | Start here | Check before relying on it | Backup |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Find a place or route | Naver Map or Kakao Map basic search | Account requirements can differ for saving, booking or paying | Save the Korean address and route screenshot |
| Book a taxi | k.ride’s visitor-facing signup | Your country code, account method and accepted payment option | Use an official taxi rank and confirm payment with the driver |
| Reserve a restaurant | CATCHTABLE Global | Correct branch, availability and deposit terms | Ask the restaurant about direct booking or walk-ins |
| Join a restaurant queue | The venue’s global waitlist or its staff | Remote versus on-site registration and how you will be notified | Stay nearby and ask staff how to monitor your turn |
| Order food or shop online | The service’s current visitor checkout, if offered | Address, contact number, identity check and card acceptance separately | Buy in person or ask your accommodation about available assistance |
| Open a resident financial service | The provider’s foreign-resident process | Accepted residence documents and verification requirements | Ask the provider which in-person route is available |

This is a task checklist, not a guarantee that every account or card will be accepted. In particular, a map listing is not evidence that the linked booking service uses the same signup rules.

CATCHTABLE’s [official app description](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/catchtable-book-restaurants/id1639046576) explicitly offers bookings without a Korean number. Kakao Mobility’s [k.ride announcement](https://www.kakaomobility.com/newsroom/detail/카카오모빌리티-외국인-전용-모빌리티-플랫폼-케이라이드kride--미국일본주요-동남아-등-해외-12개국으로-서비스-확대-251) describes phone-number entry followed by Google, Apple or email authentication and support for overseas-issued cards. Complete setup while you have reliable Wi-Fi and time to solve an error.

## When a screen rejects you

1. **Identify the step.** Is the problem creating an account, receiving a code, passing 본인인증, or paying? Write down the exact error.
2. **Check the version.** Look for the service’s official global or visitor offering. A translated domestic page may still require domestic verification.
3. **Check the number field.** If supported, select your actual country code. Do not enter a made-up Korean number or someone else’s identity details.
4. **Check the payment separately.** Reaching checkout does not prove your issuer will authorize the transaction. Look for the international-card option and inspect your bank’s authentication request.
5. **Use a practical fallback.** Ask the restaurant or service provider which alternative is available. For tonight’s dinner, a walk-in or takeaway can be easier than resolving an account restriction.

Keep your error screenshot private if it contains identity or payment details. Share only the relevant information with official support.

## Prepare before landing

Save your accommodation’s Korean address, install your chosen map and taxi apps, and sign into any restaurant booking account you expect to use. Check that you can still access the email or phone number used for account recovery. Keep booking confirmations available offline, but reconnect to monitor live waitlists.

## If you are moving here

Visitor workarounds cover a trip; a resident setup has different needs. Ask your carrier whether your exact plan and registered identity support SMS verification and PASS. The [PASS FAQ](https://www.passauth.co.kr/question) allows foreigners who meet its phone-authentication and app-registration conditions; it does not promise that every foreigner or tourist SIM qualifies.
