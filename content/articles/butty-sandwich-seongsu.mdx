Sandwiches, smoothies and a leafy garden make Butty a welcoming lunch stop in Seongsu.

## A leafy corner of Seongsu

Before you look at the menu, look at the entrance. White pillars frame dark brickwork, climbing greenery spills over the balcony, and flowers fill the little garden beside a wooden SANDWICH sign. Butty Sandwich House has the kind of frontage that makes a walk through Seongsu's lanes feel like a discovery.

Olive-colored doors, wood counters and pale tiles give the interior a warm, understated feel. Outside, a small seating area sits among the plants. Butty fits naturally into a day of exploring Seongsu: stop for a sandwich between shops, or take a little longer over lunch in the garden.

## Inside: watch the sandwich counter at work

The open counter is a focal point of the room. Behind the glass, leafy greens, shredded carrots, sliced fillings and bottles of sauce are arranged within reach of the staff. Bread and stacks of plates sit above them, while warm lamps soften the exposed ceiling and tiled surfaces.

Sandwiches are the focus, with options ranging from jambon to cheese-filled panini. The menu makes it easy to choose between a ham-based meal, a hot mushroom sandwich and a basil-and-tomato alternative. Add a smoothie for a fuller lunch, or keep things simple with a sandwich before continuing your walk.

## Garden seating or takeaway?

Tables and benches sit close to the garden planting, beneath the building's white balcony. The outdoor area is an inviting spot for a break in comfortable weather. If you prefer eating inside, ask staff about available seating when you arrive.

Takeaway is another option if you want to continue exploring the neighborhood. Keep some flexibility in your itinerary: visitor reports mention busy periods and waits, so allow time for your order even when taking it to go. See [Polle's visitor reports](https://polle.com/place/59morM/%EB%B2%84%ED%8B%B0%20%EC%83%8C%EB%93%9C%EC%9C%84%EC%B9%98%20%ED%95%98%EC%9A%B0%EC%8A%A4).
