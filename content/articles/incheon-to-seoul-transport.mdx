You have just landed, you are tired, and there are four ways into Seoul all shouting for your attention. The good news: they are all safe, clean and easy. The choice really comes down to your luggage, your budget, where your hotel is, and what time it is. Here is the honest breakdown.

## The quick verdict

Take the **AREX train** unless you have a reason not to — it is fast, cheap, and immune to Seoul's famous traffic. Take the **airport limousine bus** if you have heavy bags and your hotel sits on a direct route, because you never touch your luggage until your stop. Take a **taxi** when it is late, when you are splitting the fare, or when you simply cannot face a transfer. Skip the idea of a KTX from the airport — there is no direct high-speed line into central Seoul, so the train option is AREX.

## At a glance

| Option | Fare (one way) | Time to central Seoul | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AREX all-stop train | ~₩9,500 | 56–60 min (13 stops) | Budget, hotels near a subway line |
| AREX express train | ₩13,000 | ~43 min to Seoul Station | Speed, a reserved seat, Seoul Station area |
| Airport limousine bus | ₩17,000–18,000 | 60–90 min (traffic) | Big luggage, a hotel on the route |
| Metered taxi | ~₩55,000–80,000 + ₩7,900 toll | 60–90 min (traffic) | Late night, groups, door-to-door |

## Option by option

### AREX (Airport Railroad Express)

Two trains run on the same track from the basement (B1) of both terminals. The **all-stop** train (₩9,500) makes 13 stops including Gimpo Airport, Hongik University (Hongdae) and Digital Media City before Seoul Station, and you can tap in with a T-money card. The **express** train (₩13,000, sometimes ~₩11,500 pre-booked online) runs non-stop to Seoul Station in about 43 minutes with a reserved seat. Both are punctual and comfortable. The catch: at Seoul Station or your stop you may still need one subway transfer to reach your hotel, dragging bags through the station.

### Airport limousine bus

Buses leave from the ground-floor curb (look for the numbered stands outside gates 4–9 in Terminal 1). Standard deluxe routes such as the 6001 and 6015 cost about ₩17,000, and premium K-Limousine or KAL routes about ₩18,000. You buy a ticket at the kiosk, the driver stows your suitcase under the coach, and you ride in a big reclining seat straight to a stop near major hotels and districts. The downside is traffic: a jam on the expressway can push the trip past 90 minutes.

### Taxi

Regular (orange or silver) metered taxis run roughly ₩55,000–80,000 to central Seoul; **deluxe** black taxis are ₩80,000–120,000 with more room and English-speaking drivers; **international taxis** offer flat fares around ₩55,000–75,000 booked at a dedicated desk. Every airport taxi adds the ₩7,900 Incheon Expressway toll, and a 20% night surcharge applies between midnight and 4am. It is the only true door-to-door option and the only one that always runs, even at 3am.

## Costs compared

For a solo traveler to a central Seoul hotel, the train is dramatically cheaper; for a group of three or four with luggage, a single taxi can actually beat four bus tickets.

| Scenario | Cheapest choice | Rough total |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solo, hotel near a subway stop | AREX all-stop | ~₩9,500 |
| Solo, hotel near Seoul Station | AREX express | ₩13,000 |
| Solo, big suitcase, hotel on a bus route | Limousine bus | ₩17,000–18,000 |
| 3–4 people, lots of luggage | One taxi, split | ~₩60,000–90,000 total |
| After midnight | Taxi (only option) | ~₩70,000–100,000 with night rate |

You can pay for the train and bus with a T-money card — well worth setting up first. See the [T-money card guide](/getting-around/t-money-card-guide) and, if you want app-based rides in the city, our [Korea taxi apps guide](/getting-around/korea-taxi-apps).

## Which should you choose?

- **Light bags, budget-minded, staying near any subway line:** AREX all-stop. Best value in the country.
- **Want it fast and simple, staying near Seoul Station:** AREX express.
- **Two big suitcases and a hotel the bus stops near:** limousine bus, every time — no stairs, no transfers.
- **Landing after the last train, or three of you splitting the cost:** taxi.

Whatever you pick, grab your SIM and T-money card in the arrival hall first — see the full [Incheon arrival guide](/first-72-hours/incheon-airport-arrival-guide) for the order of operations before you even reach the platform.
